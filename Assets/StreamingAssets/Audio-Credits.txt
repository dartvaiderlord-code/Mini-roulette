Carousel sound design

Midnight Waltz is an original composition for Carousel.

Report, Click and Ratchet are edited mixes of Red Eclipse's pistol primary, primaryfar2 and reload recordings and sfx/shell2. Fracture, Scatter and Spill combine the project's debris2, splashin, splashout2 and waterfall_indoor with additional edits. Changes include trimming, layering, pitch changes, filtering and room reflections. Red Eclipse Team (including Q009, xJoJox and Quinton Reeves); ambient sound credits include Quinton Reeves and Alex Anguix. The six adapted clips are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://github.com/redeclipse/sounds/tree/ae74727c8e56a4c1d0638d2eb3e9d016cdd9cade
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Fracture and Scatter also incorporate an edited portion of dsslop.wav from Freedoom.
